Player Help
Everything you can do on this portal, in 60 seconds each.
1. What is this?

This is your player portal. Pick your name at the top, then:

  • 📅 Availability — tell your captain which games you can play
  • 💬 Chat — message your captain and teammates
  • 🔔 Notifications — get pinged about games, chats, scores
  • 📸 Photos — share photos and videos with your team
2. Add to your home screen

Works like an app when installed.

  • iPhone (Safari) — Share button → Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome) — 3-dot menu → Install app.

iPhone push notifications only work when installed to home screen.

3. Don't see your name?

You're not on the roster yet. Register here and your captain will approve you.

Captain Help
Quick rundown of everything you can do here.
1. Submit Game Scores

After each game, one captain (either side) submits the final score. The other captain confirms. Once both agree, the game is marked final and the league-wide scores/standings/stats update automatically.

For the full box score tool (batting, pitching, innings), use Open Full Box Score Tool in the Submit Score tab.

2. Track Payments

In the Payments tab, tap the checkbox next to each player's name as they pay. Adam can see paid totals for league accounting.

3. Team Chat & Captains Chat

Team Chat — messages go to your roster only. Players with notifications on get pinged.

Captains Chat — private group chat with all captains + the commissioner. Great for game-day coordination, rainouts, etc.

4. Availability

The Attendance tab shows you each player's Yes / Maybe / No for every upcoming game. Players set these from their own portal.
