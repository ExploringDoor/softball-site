General Liability Release and Medical Release

The Undersigned further attests, affirms, warrants and certifies that (s)he is covered by a personal or family medical plan (including, but not necessarily limited to, hospitalization, dental, and the like), which would fully cover, reimburse and/or indemnify the Undersigned for any and all injuries, of any nature, character or type, which could or may possibly result from my participation in the said activities and Program hereof. However, in the event that I do incur any medical bills/expenses as a result of my participation in the said activities and Program, then the said medical bills/expenses shall be paid by me as my sole responsibility to do so and, thus, I hereby release, acquit, discharge and/or otherwise indemnify the DVSL and the Team (including, but not necessarily limited to, all of the management personnel of both the DVSL and the Team (including the DVSL Commissioners as well as the Captain/Manager of the above-stated Team), its associated and respective personnel, members, organizers and the like) against any and all medical or dental claims, of any nature, type, kind or character, that may be made by me or on my behalf.

DVSL Rule 1 — Eligibility Certification

To be eligible to play on any team in the DVSL, a player must:

  1. be Jewish or a member of a Jewish household; and
  2. be either:
    1. twenty-one (21) years of age or older; or
    2. an "Underage Player", defined to be sixteen (16) years of age at the beginning of the season OR the underage player becomes eligible once they turn 16 during the present league season — and with a parent on the roster of the same DVSL team as the Underage Player — and with a parent playing in the lineup of the DVSL team in any game in which the Underage Player is playing on the same team;
  3. comply with one (or more) of the following:
    1. be a full-time dues paying member of a synagogue; or
    2. have a child in a Jewish Day School program; or
    3. be a full-time employee of Goldstein's; or
    4. make a verifiable charitable donation to a Jewish charity or Jewish organization in an amount of $100 or greater.

The Undersigned further affirms, warrants, acknowledges and confirms his/her understanding that DVSL Rule 1, governing Eligibility, is and will be actively enforced, and should it be determined that (s)he does not meet the Eligibility Requirements set forth in DVSL Rule 1 and as certified to herein, penalties shall be imposed upon the Undersigned, the Undersigned's Team, the Undersigned Team's Captain, and/or other players on the Undersigned's Team.
