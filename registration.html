Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know about joining DVSL.
How do I pay team dues? ⌄
After submitting this form, the commissioner will reach out with payment instructions. Dues can typically be paid by check or Venmo. Payment must be received before the season begins.
What is a free agent? ⌄
Free agents are individual players looking to join a team. We'll try to match you with a team that needs players. Submit your info and we'll be in touch if a spot opens up.
When is the registration deadline? ⌄
Registration typically closes a few weeks before the season starts. Submit early to guarantee your spot — teams fill up fast.
What fields do you play at? ⌄
DVSL uses multiple fields across the Delaware Valley area. Check the Field Guide for a full list of venues and their specific ground rules.
Can I register a new team? ⌄
Yes! Select "Team Registration" above. New teams go through an approval process. The commissioner will be in touch to discuss division placement and confirm your registration.
Who do I contact with questions? ⌄
Email the commissioner directly at toddharris1222@gmail.com or submit this form and we'll get back to you.
