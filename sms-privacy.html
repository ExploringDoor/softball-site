Text program privacy

What DVSL keeps when you sign up for texts, and what it never does with it. Last updated November 20, 2026.

What we collect

  • Your cell phone number, your name, your team and whether you are a player, captain or fan.
  • Your consent: the exact words you agreed to, when you agreed, the web address you signed up from and your browser, and when you replied YES.
  • A log of the texts DVSL sends you and the texts you send DVSL (only the last four digits of your number are kept in that log), and whether each text was delivered.

How we use it

Only to run the DVSL text program described in the Text Program Terms: texting you about your team's games, posting the scores captains text in, and showing that you agreed to the texts. The consent you gave was:

I agree to receive text messages from DVSL (Delaware Valley Softball League) at this number about my team's games: messages from my team's captain and the league office, a text when one of my team's games is moved, postponed or cancelled, and replies to the scores and questions I text. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Agreeing is not a condition of registering or playing.

Never sold or shared

Your mobile number and your opt-in and consent information are never sold, rented or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. The only outside company that receives your number is our text delivery provider, Twilio, and only so it can deliver DVSL texts. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Who can see it

The DVSL league office can see who signed up for each team. Captains see how many teammates signed up, not their numbers. Nothing about the text program is shown on any public page.

Opting out and deleting your number

Reply STOP to any DVSL text and we stop texting you. To have your number removed from our records, email DVSLCommissioner@gmail.com.

Security

Sign-ups are stored in the league's private database, which only the league office and the website's own server can read.

Who runs it

The program is run for DVSL by Always Competing Sports LLC, doing business as Mainline Web Design, on the league's behalf. It follows this policy.

Contact

DVSL league office: DVSLCommissioner@gmail.com.
