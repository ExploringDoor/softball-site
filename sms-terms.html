Text program terms

How the DVSL text message program works, and your choices. Last updated November 20, 2026.

What this is

DVSL (Delaware Valley Softball League) runs a text message program so players, captains and fans can keep up with their team's games. When you sign up you may receive:

  • A text when one of your team's games is moved to a new date, time or field, postponed, cancelled or put back on.
  • Messages about your team's games from your team's captain or the DVSL league office.
  • Replies to the scores and questions you text to the DVSL number. Captains on the league's list can text in final scores.

That is the whole program. There is no marketing or advertising.

How to join

Sign up on the DVSL text sign-up page: enter your name, cell number and team, and tick the box beside these words:

I agree to receive text messages from DVSL (Delaware Valley Softball League) at this number about my team's games: messages from my team's captain and the league office, a text when one of my team's games is moved, postponed or cancelled, and replies to the scores and questions I text. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Agreeing is not a condition of registering or playing.

You then get one text asking you to reply YES. Your number is signed up only after you reply YES. Agreeing to the texts is never a condition of registering or playing.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with your team's schedule and the weather. Message and data rates may apply, charged by your mobile carrier, not by DVSL.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any DVSL text at any time. You get one text confirming you are out, and then no more texts unless you sign up again (reply START to come back).

Getting help

Reply HELP to any DVSL text, or email DVSLCommissioner@gmail.com.

Carriers and delivery

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery depends on your carrier's network and your phone's settings.

Who runs it

The program is run for DVSL by Always Competing Sports LLC, doing business as Mainline Web Design, which builds and operates the DVSL website and its text messaging on the league's behalf. Messages come from the DVSL text number and are about DVSL games only.

Changes

We may update these terms. The current version is always the one on this page. How we handle your number is in the Text Program Privacy Policy.

Contact

DVSL league office: DVSLCommissioner@gmail.com.
