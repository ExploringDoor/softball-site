Ground rules supplement — and do not supersede — the official rulebook. Umpires hold final authority.
The following ground rules govern play at all DVSL venues for the 2025 season. Each venue presents unique physical characteristics — walkways, berms, trees, and adjacent property — requiring specific rulings beyond the standard rulebook. Umpires hold final authority on all ground rule disputes during play.
Mondauk Common Park
2 Fields · Ambler, PA
Field 1
Mondauk 4
1452 Dillon Road, Ambler, PA 19034
Mondauk Common
1st BaseRight Field Line
90° perpendicular line from the end of the dugout fence to the base of the berm/hill, continuing straight in line with the base of the berm.
3rd BaseLeft Field Line
Line from end of dugout fence to the point on the walk path where the path curves to the right.
Special Rules
Home RunA batted ball that rolls onto the walk path in Left Field is ruled a home run.
Fly BallIf a fly ball strikes the overhanging tree in Left Field, ball is immediately dead. If it would have been foul — charged a foul ball. If fair — no penalty; ball is replayed.
Thrown BallIf a thrown ball strikes the overhanging tree in Left Field, ball remains live. All runners may advance at will.
Field 2
Mondauk 5
1452 Dillon Road, Ambler, PA 19034
Mondauk Common
1st BaseRight Field Line
Line from end of dugout fence to the point on the walk path where the curve goes right, then continuing straight along the left side of the walk path.
3rd BaseLeft Field Line
Line from end of dugout fence to the point on the walk path equal to the infield cutout, then continuing straight along the walk path.
Special Rules — Right Field Swale
GR DoubleA fair batted ball entering the swale or crossing the walk path down the RF line is dead. Each runner awarded 2 bases from position when hit.
Thrown BallA thrown ball entering the swale or crossing the walk path is dead. Each runner awarded 2 bases from position when throw left fielder's hand.
Deflected BallA deflected ball entering the swale or crossing the walk path is dead. Each runner awarded 2 bases from position at time of deflection.
Southampton
1 Field · Southampton, PA
Field 3
Schaefer Park
444 Gravel Hill Road, Southampton, PA 18966
Southampton
1st BaseRight Field Line
Line from end of fence to the light pole, then continuing straight.
3rd BaseLeft Field Line
Line from end of fence to shrub/tree growth, then continuing straight.
Special Rules
Variable BoundaryThe out-of-play boundary down the 3rd base/LF line is usually fence to light pole — unless tree or shrub growth has encroached upon the field. If encroachment exists, ground rules must be adjusted prior to the start of play.
