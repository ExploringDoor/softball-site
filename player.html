1. What is this?

This is your player portal — a private page for your team. Once you've picked your team and entered your team password, pick your name at the top and you can do three things:

  • 📅 Availability — tell your captain which games you can play
  • 📸 Photos — share photos and videos with your team
  • 💬 Team Chat — message your captain and teammates

Your captain gets your availability and messages instantly. No texts, no group chats to manage.

2. Add to your home screen

This site works like an app when you add it to your phone's home screen.

  • iPhone (Safari) — tap the Share button at the bottom, then "Add to Home Screen."
  • Android (Chrome) — tap the 3-dot menu, then "Add to Home screen" or "Install app."

After that, the DVSL icon lives next to your other apps. Opens faster, no address bar, and push notifications only work when installed to the home screen on iPhone.

3. Mark your game availability

On the Availability tab you'll see every upcoming game for your team. For each one, tap:

  • ✓ In — I'll be there
  • ✗ Out — I can't make it
  • ? Maybe — I'm not sure yet

You can change your answer anytime — just tap again. Your captain sees the tally on their dashboard so they know if they need to line up subs.

4. Post photos and videos

On the Photos tab, any teammate can post:

  • Photos — pick an image from your camera roll, add an optional caption, hit Share.
  • YouTube videos — paste a YouTube link and it embeds right in the feed.

Only your team can see what you post here. If push notifications are on, your teammates get a little ping when someone uploads.

5. Team Chat

The Team Chat tab is a real-time group conversation with your captain and everyone on your team.

  • Send — type a message, hit Send. Teammates with notifications on get pinged.
  • Delete your own message — tap the red × that appears on your own bubbles.
  • Your captain can delete any message and reset the whole chat if it gets out of hand.
6. Push notifications Recommended

Get pinged when something happens — no need to check the app. On the main DVSL site, open the hamburger menu and tap Notifications, then "Enable."

You can turn on:

  • Team Chat — new messages from your captain and teammates
  • Schedule changes — rainouts, field moves, time changes
  • Pregame reminders — 1 hour before each of your games
  • Live scores — when a game you care about goes live
  • Photos — new team uploads
  • Commissioner announcements — rain decisions, league-wide news

iPhone users: push only works if you've added the site to your home screen first (see step 2). Android works out of the box.

7. Don't see your name?

If your name isn't in the "Who are you?" dropdown, you're not on your team's roster yet. Two ways to fix it:

  • Register yourself — open registration.html, pick your team, fill out the form. Your captain approves you.
  • Ask your captain — they can add you to the roster in their captain portal in about 10 seconds.

Once you're on the roster, your name shows up here and you can use everything above.

8. Other things you can do on DVSL.com

Beyond this portal, the main site has a lot of stuff worth bookmarking:

  • Schedule — every game for every team, with field locations and subscribe-to-calendar links.
  • Stats — league-wide hitting and pitching leaderboards. Sortable, filterable by team or split.
  • Standings — current win/loss and run differential.
  • Watch — live game scores and play-by-play while games are in progress.
  • Photos — the public team photo gallery.
  • Playoffs — bracket view once we're in October.

Tip: Your team page (reachable from Standings or Stats) has a "Subscribe to Google Calendar" link — tap it once and every schedule change for your team pushes straight to your phone's calendar.
