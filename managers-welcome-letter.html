DVSL is now an app — here's everything it does

Hi Captains,

The DVSL site now works as a full app on your phone's home screen — no App Store download, no updates to manage. Here's how to install it and what you can do with it.

The big picture: once you install this and start using it, you don't need TeamSnap, GameChanger, or IM Leagues anymore. Next season — or even this season if you want to dive in now — DVSL does all of it in one place.

📲 Install it on your home screen (iPhone / iPad)

  1. Open https://dvsl.vercel.app/ in Safari (not Chrome — the "install" flow is Safari-only on iPhone).
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari. It looks like this:
    Safari's Share button — a rounded square with an arrow coming out the top. It sits in the middle of the toolbar at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen."
  4. Tap Add in the top right. Done — DVSL is now an app on your home screen.

From now on, open the app from that icon (not the Safari bookmark). That's when you get the real app experience: no browser bar, faster loads, and push notifications.

Android users: Open the site in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu, choose "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen"). Same result.

🔔 Push notifications — just like any other app

Open the Inbox (the bell icon 🔔 at the bottom of the app) and tap Enable Notifications. Once you do, DVSL pushes alerts to your lock screen exactly like any other app on your phone — the moment something happens:

  • Team chat — the second someone on your team sends a message, every teammate's phone pings instantly. Same as a text message.
  • Captains chat — captains-only messages ping the moment they're sent.
  • Scores — as soon as a final is submitted, you get a push with the recap.
  • Rainouts / Schedule changes — pinged the moment I mark a game rained out or moved.
  • Announcements — league-wide news lands on your lock screen.
  • Pre-game reminders — an automatic 1-hour-out nudge before your game.
  • Admin alerts — for captains, score-conflict pings and league housekeeping.

Everything that pushes also lands back in that same Inbox 🔔 at the bottom — tap it any time to see what you haven't read. Tap an item and it takes you straight to that game or conversation.

💬 Team Chat + Captains Chat

  • Team chat — private chat for your team's roster.
  • Captains chat — direct line between captains. Use it to coordinate reschedules, ask about rules calls, or lobby the commissioner.
  • Every message shows the sender's real first + last name, so threads are legible.

For captains: your dashboard

  • Roster management — edit jersey numbers, positions (including LCF/RCF), and sub-outs right from your phone.
  • Submit box scores as a PDF — upload the PDF of your scorecard and the app pre-fills the box score for you to review and submit. No more typing stats line-by-line.
  • Captains-only chat — always-on line to me and the other captains.
  • Your team's notifications — test push to your own phone, see who on your roster has alerts turned on.
