Text updates

Get DVSL texts

Players, captains and fans can get a text when their team's game is rained out, moved or cancelled, and when their captain or the league office sends the team a message. Sign up once with your cell phone.

What you will get

  • A text when one of your team's games is moved to a new date, time or field, postponed, cancelled or put back on.
  • Messages about your team's games from your captain or the DVSL league office.
  • Replies to the scores and questions you text to the DVSL number.
  • Nothing else. No marketing, and your number is never sold or shared.
I am a

After you sign up you get one text from DVSL. Reply YES to it to finish. Until you reply YES, you get nothing else. Reply STOP to any DVSL text to opt out.

Captains: text your score

Captains on the DVSL list can text a final score to the DVSL text number and it goes straight onto the site, for example OA 12 SA 9 or we won 12-9 or rained out. Reply MENU to see what else it understands.

Questions: DVSLCommissioner@gmail.com. Read the Text Program Terms and the Text Program Privacy Policy.
